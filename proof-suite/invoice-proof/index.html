Privacy and evidence scope

The invoice file, invoice number, date, amount, currency and party names are processed in your browser only. The blockchain receives a one-way SHA-256 fingerprint, the submitting wallet address and the transaction timestamp. A match proves that the supplied file and details reproduce the registered fingerprint; it does not prove payment, delivery, tax compliance, authenticity of the issuer or legal validity.
