Four separate evidence levels

1. Integrity record: the file fingerprint and SOBYA block record can show that matching bytes existed by the recorded block. The block time is not automatically a legally qualified timestamp.

2. Wallet signer: the transaction identifies the signing wallet address. It does not establish the person's civil identity or legal ownership. A separate, explicit signed declaration is a planned next phase and is not part of this MVP.

3. Verified creator identity: this will require a trusted identity issuer and a validated credential. It is not available yet. Never publish identity documents or personal data on-chain.

4. Legal attestation: this requires evidence from an authorized provider, notary, registry, or qualified e-signature service under the relevant law. A SOBYA record alone does not confer legal title or guarantee court recognition.

Current status: only the integrity record and wallet transaction address are supported in this MVP. Identity and legal verification are not yet implemented.

What this proves — and what it does not

A matching fingerprint shows that the selected file has the same bytes as the file fingerprint recorded in this transaction. It does not prove who created the file, that its contents are true, or that the signer had legal ownership.

The fingerprint and wallet address are public on-chain. File names and file contents are not sent. You pay the SOBYA network fee shown by your wallet when registering.
