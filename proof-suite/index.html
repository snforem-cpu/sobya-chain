WHY SOBYA PROOF SUITE

What these tools do — and why they matter

The three services work together to create records that can be checked later without relying only on a sender's copy or a private database.

01

File integrity

A SHA-256 fingerprint helps detect whether a document has changed since its proof was recorded. The original file remains on your device during registration.

02

Invoice traceability

Link an invoice file to its reference, date, amount and currency through one fingerprint, making later comparisons easier for counterparties.

03

Independent verification

A public SOBYA Mainnet transaction gives reviewers a shared reference. The API also lets businesses automate checks in their own systems.

Privacy, evidence and trust

Files and invoice details are processed in your browser; the tools publish a one-way fingerprint and the submitting wallet address as part of the SOBYA Mainnet record. Anyone with the transaction reference can independently inspect the record. This creates a tamper-evident reference, not a certificate that the document is true.

A matching fingerprint confirms that the supplied bytes and recorded fingerprint match. By itself, it does not prove authorship, a person's civil identity, legal ownership, invoice payment, delivery, tax compliance or the truth of the document's contents.
